# Makoma / M’AKOMA research notes

Research captured 2026-10-29 from the public official site. Search results also surfaced similarly named businesses, so this website is grounded specifically in the M’AKOMA beaded-technology brand at makoma.io, not Makoma House.

- M’AKOMA describes itself as jewelry-first beaded technology: six person beads represent a chosen circle, with no screen, feed, step count, or ordinary notifications. Source: https://makoma.io/the-piece and https://makoma.io/
- Official product copy describes holding a bead, speaking, and tapping to send a voice message. The bead gives a light/pulse cue; the product is not presented as a smartwatch or fitness tracker. Source: https://makoma.io/the-piece and https://makoma.io/preorder
- Official page says the beads carry Adinkra symbols from Ghana and lists eight meanings: Sankofa (return and retrieve), Nkonsonkonson (linked together), Akoma Ntoaso (linked hearts), Akoma (the heart), Gye Nyame (except God), Aya (the fern), Nsoroma (child of the heavens), and Nkyinkyim (the winding path). Source: https://makoma.io/the-piece
- The official preorder page describes one hand-built bracelet with six person beads, splash- and dust-resistance (IP54), and an iPhone/Android app. Availability, shipping and price can change; the website should link to the official preorder page rather than repeat time-sensitive details.
- Official site identifies M’AKOMA as an early-stage company, hand-built in small batches, built by Melvin Osei Opoku. Source: https://makoma.io/the-piece
